# Product Vision

## North star

Kill Tide should become the trusted personal companion that helps people understand when to enter the water and remember what happened there.

The immediate objective is concrete:

> Build a beautiful tool Isabella can use to log her next spearfishing dive.

The long-term opportunity is broader:

> Create a personalized marine-window intelligence platform built on trustworthy data, local knowledge, and a lifetime of real ocean experience.

## Origin

The name came from a family conversation in which “kill tide” described the resting period around a tidal transition: the moment when a spearfisher can move without fighting a strong current.

The formal marine concept is generally **slack water**. Slack water concerns tidal-current direction and does not necessarily coincide with high or low water. Local geography can create a delay between water-level changes and current changes.

That distinction defines the future product:

> Most tide products tell people where the water will be. Kill Tide should help them understand when the water slows down—and whether that window is worth using.

“Kill” is not a statement of violence or disrespect. In the brand story it represents a decisive window: prepare patiently, read the conditions, and move when the opportunity arrives.

## Product promise

Kill Tide combines two connected jobs:

1. Help a diver decide when conditions may be favorable.
2. Preserve the evidence of what actually happened.

A complete history may include location, conditions, catches, gear, wildlife, photographs, lessons, safety observations, and memories. Over time, this personal evidence can answer questions that generic forecasting products cannot.

## Initial audience

The first user is a spearfisher who wants a fast, visual, private journal after leaving the water.

The marine-planning system may later serve freedivers, anglers, paddlers, boaters, photographers, and coastal workers. Product expansion should follow demonstrated needs without weakening the spearfishing roots of the brand.

## Brand foundation

**Tagline:** Move when the ocean gives you the opportunity.

Kill Tide stands for:

- Patience and timing
- Preparation and discipline
- Ocean intelligence
- Quiet confidence
- Experience over ego
- Respect for marine life
- Conservation and community

The visual identity is cinematic, restrained, rugged, and premium. It uses near-black charcoal, deep navy, slate blue, weathered ivory, and muted seafoam. It avoids cartoon fish, pirate imagery, skulls, blood, tactical styling, clichés, and loud effects.

## Business direction

The first business is a useful product and a trusted brand—not apparel.

Possible paid capabilities may eventually include advanced planning, unlimited locations, automated calendars, personalized thresholds, notifications, synchronization, groups, or professional tools. Merchandise may express community loyalty later; it should not substitute for product value.

## Product principles

1. Start with a real personal need.
2. Make the first workflow complete before adding breadth.
3. Keep the proof of concept local, private, and free.
4. Treat every dive as valuable, including no-catch dives.
5. Protect exact locations by default.
6. Never present invented marine data as fact.
7. Show sources, confidence, and uncertainty.
8. Use AI to interpret evidence, not manufacture it.
9. Introduce cloud infrastructure only when usage justifies it.
10. Let merchandise follow community—not lead the company.
